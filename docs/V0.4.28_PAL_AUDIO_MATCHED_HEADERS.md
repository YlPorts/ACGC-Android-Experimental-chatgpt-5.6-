# v0.4.28 - PAL regional audio with matched headers

The earlier whole-file `audiorom.img` experiment could crash because the USA
binary kept the USA `AudioseqHeaderStart`, `AudiobankHeaderStart`,
`AudiowaveHeaderStart`, and `AudiodataHeaderStart` tables while reading a PAL
audio image whose internal offsets and sizes differ.

This revision treats regional audio as an inseparable pair:

- `languages/<code>/audio/audiorom.img`
- `languages/<code>/audio/audio_headers.bin`

`audio_headers.bin` is the 0x1A50-byte block containing the four matching PAL
ArcHeader tables (sequence, bank, wave, data) extracted from that language's PAL
executable. The common Audio map table is identical across USA and PAL Multi5
and remains compiled in the port.

At startup the port validates:

- exact header block size and expected entry counts (249 / 159 / 6 / 3),
- medium/cache fields,
- every sequence/bank/wave entry against its section bounds,
- the three top-level data sections are contiguous, and
- the final section end exactly matches the selected `audiorom.img` length.

Only after all checks pass are the PAL headers applied and the external audio
image exposed to the DVD layer. Missing, damaged, or mismatched files fall back
to the USA audio and headers instead of mixing regional layouts.

The language/text/graphics pack remains independent: invalid or absent regional
audio never disables the translated text resources.
